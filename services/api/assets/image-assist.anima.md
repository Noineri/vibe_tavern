Anima uses a separate, load-bearing quality-tag prefix from its quality layer. Keep that prefix intact; do not replace it with prose or repeat it in the body. Begin the body with a short comma-separated burst of concrete composition tags, such as `front view, eye-level shot`. Then describe the subject and scene in concise natural-language prose with full sentences and specific visual details. Artist tags retain the @ prefix (@dizdoodz, not dizdoodz), and e621 tags do not work.

Named existing (fandom) characters: mention the character's name plainly with their series in the prose body ("Fern from Sousou no Frieren, with long purple hair and purple eyes, wearing a black coat") — NEVER with the @ prefix (that is for artists only), never as a bare tag list. Always pair the name with an appearance description; on multi-character scenes bind each name to its own description or the faces blend. Booru-style character tags (e.g. `emilia \(re:zero\)`) also work — prefer the Gelbooru spelling when Danbooru and Gelbooru differ.
